I'm a third year Ph.D. student at the Centre for Ibero-American Studies at Charles University and researcher at the Institute of Sociological Studies at Charles University within the research project _Beyond Security: Role of Conflict in Resilience-Building_. 

My regional focus is on Latin America and Central and Eastern Europe, and how these two regions' similar experiences as third wave democracies shape their current democratic experiences. My research interests include electoral behavior, political parties, institutionalism and their gendered aspects.

My recently published articles include research on legislative turnover in Latin America and the Latin American legislators dataset. The Latin American legislators dataset was created out of necessity for a comprehensive dataset that includes names of all elected legislators in the regions since the onset of the third wave of democratization. The dataset is baised on the FAIR principle of being Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable and is available at the OSF under the following link: https://osf.io/nk4ma.
